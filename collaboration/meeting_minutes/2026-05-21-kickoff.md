## Initial EDA Discussion

The group agreed that each EDA notebook should go beyond simple column summaries and clearly identify:

- the level or grain of each table;
- primary keys and join keys;
- duplicate rows and duplicate business keys;
- missing-value patterns;
- whether the table is a dimension, fact, bridge, or history table;
- whether the table can safely support the student's final modelling grain.
